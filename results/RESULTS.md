# Results

Held-out evaluation on 10 traffic seeds (1000-1009), never seen in training. Values are mean ± half-width of the 95% bootstrap CI. Generated by `experiments/evaluate.py`.

**Metrics.** Wait = time stopped, for vehicles that entered the network. Delay = time stopped plus time queued before entering, for every vehicle, including those still outside at the end. The new junctions (four-way, Lusaka) are judged on delay, because a controller that starves an arm can push its queue outside the network, where wait would not see it.
