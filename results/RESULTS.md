# Results

Held-out evaluation on 10 traffic seeds (1000-1009), never seen in training. Every learner was trained in 5 independent runs, and all 5 are evaluated. Values are mean ± half-width of the 95% bootstrap CI; for learners the bootstrap resamples both training runs and traffic seeds. Every episode has a 300 s warm-up that the metrics leave out. Generated by `experiments/evaluate.py`.

**Metrics.** Wait = time stopped, for vehicles that entered the network. Delay = time stopped plus time queued before entering, for every vehicle, including those still outside at the end. The new junctions (four-way, Manda Hill) are judged on delay, because a controller that starves an arm can push its queue outside the network, where wait would not see it.

**Fixed-time plans.** "Hand-set" is the plan stored in the network. "Webster" is timed with Webster's method from saturation flows measured in SUMO (`experiments/webster.py`, `results/webster.json`); it is the fair fixed-time baseline.
